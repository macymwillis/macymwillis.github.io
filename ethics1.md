## Paper Overview

This paper explores the ethical challenges of artificial intelligence (AI) in web development and 
graphic design. It discusses job displacement, intellectual property, algorithmic bias, and 
accountability.

The paper examines how AI tools can improve efficiency and productivity while raising concerns 
about human creativity, copyright, fairness, and responsibility. It also compares Christian 
ethical principles with the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) Code of Ethics and the 
Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) Code of Ethics.

The paper emphasizes the importance of using AI responsibly, protecting human creativity,
reducing bias, respecting intellectual property, and prioritizing human well-being.

## Key Topics

- Ethical use of artificial intelligence
- Job displacement and automation
- Intellectual property and copyright
- Algorithmic bias and discrimination
- Christian ethical principles
- ACM and IEEE professional codes of ethics
- Accountability and responsible AI development
